Kadyr Myrza Ali

Prominent Kazakh Poet, Writer, and People's Writer of Kazakhstan

About Kadyr Myrza Ali

Kadyr Myrza Ali (1935–2011) was a distinguished Kazakh poet, writer, and translator who left a profound mark on Kazakh literature. Authoring over 50 books, poetry collections, and literary essays, he was honored as a People's Writer of Kazakhstan. He co-authored the text of the first National Anthem of independent Kazakhstan and translated countless works of world literature into Kazakh, playing a vital role in preserving and developing national culture.

1935 – 2011

Key Life Events

  1. 1935: Born in Dzhambeita, West Kazakhstan Region.
  2. 1958: Graduated from Kazakh State University in Almaty.
  3. 1992: Co-authored the text of the first Kazakhstani National Anthem.
  4. 1996: Awarded the title of People's Writer of Kazakhstan.

Key Works & Contributions

  • Author of over 50 poetry, prose, and children's books
  • Co-author of the first National Anthem of Kazakhstan
  • Translations of world literature (Heine, Hugo, Lermontov) into Kazakh
  • Laureate of the State Prize of the Kazakh SSR
